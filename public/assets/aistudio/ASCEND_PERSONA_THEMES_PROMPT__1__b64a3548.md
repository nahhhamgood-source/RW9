## THE GOAL
The user opens ASCEND because it feels premium, personal and a little cinematic, like his own black "operating system", and because there is a small daily thing waiting for him that he enjoys (the Daily Cut). No guilt, no pressure. Everything here is optional and switchable in Settings.

## HARD RULES FOR THIS MODULE (read twice)
1. **No copyrighted material.** Do NOT quote dialogue or lines from films, series or books, do NOT use posters, screenshots, logos, character art or character names as artwork. Describe ideas and lessons in the app's own words ("a scene where a character realises...") and reference titles only as plain text.
2. **Real people (Khabib Nurmagomedov, David Goggins, Charles Oliveira and any others):** never invent quotes, stats, dates or events. Only use widely known, publicly documented habits and values, written as a paraphrase and labelled "paraphrase, not a quote". If unsure about a fact, leave it out. No photos of them, no logos, no impersonation (the app never speaks "as" them). Show "Inspired by publicly known values. Not affiliated or endorsed."
3. **Fiction is not a how-to.** Characters here include people who are violent, criminal, manipulative or mentally unwell. The app NEVER encourages violence, crime, manipulation of others, self-harm, substance use, isolation, extreme diets or recklessness. Each path always shows three boxes: **Take this** (useful trait), **Leave this** (what not to copy), **Cost** (what it cost the character).
4. **Wellbeing:** if the user writes anything suggesting distress, hopelessness or self-harm, stop the persona voice and respond with plain care and encourage talking to someone he trusts or a professional. Paths never replace that.
5. Evidence tags stay honest: these are stories and mindset tools, not science. Tag them "Story-based idea" and "Likely helps" or "Not proven" where relevant.
6. Under-18 or unknown age: no mature-themed content details; keep the paths but show only age-appropriate summaries and the Take/Leave/Cost boxes.

## PART 3 — DAILY CUT (the reason to open the app, 30 seconds)
A short daily card, like a mini episode. One per day, shown on Today (collapsible, never required for the ring).
Format:
- **Title** and the path colour.
- **The idea (2-3 sentences, own words, spoiler-free by default):** what the character or story teaches, e.g. "A character realises preparation beats talent."
- **Your move today:** one concrete micro-action tied to an existing task (training, study block, sleep, food, face care, a conversation).
- **Reality check:** the cost or the thing not to copy.
- **Watch more (optional):** the title only, with a "Where to watch" search link (no embedded clips).
- Buttons: **Do it** (adds the micro-action), **Another** (swap), **Not for me** (hide this theme), **Save**.
Rules: 30+ lessons per path (written in the app's own words, in simple English with an optional short Arabic hint), rotating by path mix and his current focus (camp week, study, sleep), spoiler-free toggle, tone setting respected (Chill may use slang sparingly). Lessons never mention drug use, violence how-to, or crime methods. Titles are plain text only.
Also: **Saved cuts** library, **Weekly recap** ("This week's 3 ideas you acted on"), and a **Watchlist** (titles he saved) with a simple "watched" tick.

## PART 3b — DAILY MOTIVATION VIDEO (worth watching, not brain rot)
One suggested video per day, tied to his main path, shown under the Daily Cut. Goal: 5-15 minutes of something that makes him think or train better, then he closes it and goes on with his day.
- **Rules that keep it healthy:** no Shorts or Reels or endless-feed content, no autoplay, no recommended-next loop inside the app, no clips with violence glorification, no "get rich quick", "alpha" or toxic masculinity content. Prefer long-form: interviews, documentaries, technique breakdowns, talks and training sessions of about 4-20 minutes.
- **How it opens:** a button "Watch today's video" opens YouTube in a new tab (`rel="noopener noreferrer"`), or the YouTube app on the phone. Do NOT embed players, do NOT download or host any video, do NOT scrape. Offline: "Needs internet".
- **Where the video comes from (never invent URLs):**
  1. **My videos:** he can paste any YouTube link with a title and a one-line note ("why I saved it"), tag it with a path, and the app rotates through them.
  2. **Search-based suggestions:** for each path, a list of 20+ search topics (for example "Khabib discipline interview", "wrestling fundamentals tutorial", "Oliveira jiu-jitsu technique breakdown", "Goggins running mindset interview", "how to build discipline talk", "cybersecurity beginner roadmap") that open a YouTube search URL. Try the duration filter by adding the `sp` parameter for "4-20 minutes" (`sp=EgIYAw%3D%3D`); VERIFY it works and fall back to a plain search if not. Never claim a specific video exists unless he saved it.
  3. **Channels I follow:** he can save channel names to search.
- **Anti-doomscroll guardrails:** a gentle "One video a day, then done" line, an optional 10-minute timer after he taps, and a **"Did it help? Yes / No"** tap (used to rotate topics). A "Watched" tick feeds the Wins feed. A setting caps videos per day (default 1), and "Mute video suggestions" turns the feature off.
- **Make it useful:** after the video, a one-line prompt "What is one thing you will do today?" that can add a micro-action to Today.
- Respect Tone and the Arabic hint toggle for the surrounding text. Never show the video title or thumbnail inside a lock-screen notification; the daily notification just says "Today's video is ready" and is optional.
- Real people: label "Video by an external creator. ASCEND has no connection to them." Never present an uploaded video as endorsed by the person it is about.
